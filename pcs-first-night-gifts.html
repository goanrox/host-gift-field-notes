THE IDEA

Give help they can use now.

For a stateside move, ask where the family will be on arrival and what they already have. A small kit can combine practical items with a note. For an overseas PCS, shipping and baggage rules differ.

01 / ASK

Confirm the new address, dates, household size and whether they want a physical delivery.

02 / PRIORITIZE

First-night basics travel with the family, not on the moving truck.

03 / PERSONALIZE

Ask whether a local meal or unpacking help would be more useful than a physical gift.

BEFORE IT GOES

Keep the essentials off the truck.

What to carry

Military OneSource recommends carrying paperwork and medicines, plus a first-day box with toiletries, paper goods, drinks and snacks. Sheets and towels may be needed while waiting for household goods. First-day guidance ↗

What to give

A kit can help a family settle in, but it is not a substitute for their own hand-carried documents or prescriptions. Ask first instead of guessing a bed size, outlet, or delivery address.

How to send it

If sending a gift, verify the delivery window and current address with the family. For an overseas move, check shipping restrictions before placing an order; seller availability is not a promise of overseas delivery.
