A quick check before buying

Make the gift easy to receive.

For a dinner, send a quick note that says the gift is for the host, not an obligation to serve that night. If you're shipping it, check delivery at checkout rather than assuming the estimate will land before the event.

  • Ask about food allergies without spoiling the surprise, or choose a non-food gift.
  • Check the merchant's final price, shipping and substitutions.
  • Include a short gift message so the recipient knows who sent it.
